Frequently asked questions

How do I track my order?

Use the tracking number in the SMS we send when your order is dispatched. You can also email us your order number and we will tell you where it is.

Can I change or cancel my order?

Yes, as long as it has not been dispatched. Orders are usually dispatched within 24 hours, so contact us as soon as possible.

Do you do custom stitching?

Not yet. All our clothes are ready to wear in standard sizes. See the size guide to find your size.

Are your prices the same in stores?

We sell online only. We do not have physical stores.

Do you have a sale?

Our end-of-season sale runs in the first two weeks of January and July. Sale items are final sale and cannot be returned.
